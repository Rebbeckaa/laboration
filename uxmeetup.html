Join leading product designers, researchers, and interface architects for a premier day of insights, interactive sessions, and collaborative learning.

Taking place on October 14, 2027 at the Metropolitan Tech Center, Metropolitan Hall B

UX Meetup 2027 provides an exceptional venue for professionals operating within digital product design, interactive software creation, usability testing, and cross-functional user research. Over the course of this single-day conference, attendees will explore emerging trends surrounding complex workflows, scalable design systems, artificial intelligence tooling integrations, inclusive interface standards, and advanced prototyping strategies. Our goal is to host open dialogue across discipline boundaries, allowing senior leaders, practical practitioners, and aspiring designers to connect, evaluate modern software challenges, and refine their foundational methodologies together.

Additional Event Schedule Details:
Doors open for check-in and morning networking at 8:30 AM. Opening keynote begins strictly at 9:15 AM in Main Hall B. Lunch and light refreshments will be provided during the 12:30 PM break.

Agenda Highlights

01. Scaling Design Systems in Enterprise Frameworks
A deep dive into managing design components, maintaining consistency, and aligning cross-platform token structures.
02. Modern Usability Research & Continuous Testing
How agile teams integrate lightweight qualitative testing feedback cycles without delaying product releases.
03. The Intersection of AI Workflows and UI Patterns
Analyzing real-world applications of generative interfaces, predictive input fields, and dynamic user interfaces.
